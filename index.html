    How it works and what to expect

    A hand-landmark model finds 21 points on your hand. A set of rules reads finger bends and touches to name one of 10 hastas: Pataka, Tripataka, Ardhapataka, Kartarimukha, Mayura, Hamsasya, Suchi, Chandrakala, Mushti and Shikhara.

    • The rules follow common textbook descriptions. Schools differ, and a teacher's judgement beats this tool.
    • Accuracy is a prototype risk. It is checked on a small set of test hands, not on a large range of dancers, lighting and camera angles.
    • The score shown is a rule-based heuristic from 0 to 100. It is not a calibrated probability that the pose is right.
    • Works best with one hand, palm toward the camera, good light, plain background.
    • Similar pairs (Pataka and Tripataka, Suchi and Chandrakala) are the easiest to confuse.
